# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 9, 2026

11:11 Bot is designed to send scheduled reminders in Discord. It does not intentionally collect or store message content, wishes, member profiles, or personal information.

To operate, the bot processes the Discord server and channel information needed to identify where reminders should be sent. Discord and the bot's hosting provider may process limited technical information, such as connection logs, according to their own policies.

11:11 Bot does not sell or share user data for advertising. If the bot stores any operational data in the future, users may request its deletion by contacting the owner through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Kashyep).

This policy may be updated when the bot's functionality changes.
